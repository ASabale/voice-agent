# VA-SOAK-006 — Six conversations in one controller lifetime with per-turn clip attribution and VoiceBox footprint tracking

1. **ID:** VA-SOAK-006
2. **User-facing failure prevented:** A long hands-free session silently degrading — lost speech, dropped mic frames, unbounded service memory, or a controller that stops responding after many turns — none of which a single 4-turn soak can detect.
3. **Preconditions:** Live controller owning the microphone (pid 38298 at run time); VoiceBox healthy on 17493; no user speech during the window; probe owns its own isolated omp session per cycle (6 sessions, each terminated by the probe).
4. **Exact reproduction command:** `~/.local/share/omp-voice/venv/bin/python scripts/run_long_soak_probe.py` (6 cycles × 4 turns, real playback through the speakers, RSS medians of 24 samples at 5 s before and 120 s after the run, per-cycle 6-sample RSS medians, VoiceBox sidecar footprint via `footprint`, mic-counter deltas, human-transcript detection in `controller-restart.log`).
5. **Expected behavior:** every turn plays a clip generated for that turn (strict attribution: the playing clip's wav must appear in a `generated` speech event after that turn's submit); zero wake lines; zero failed speech; mic drop/invalid deltas zero; post-run RSS median within 100 MB of the baseline median; no cycle sample exceeds baseline + 300 MB; cycle medians do not trend upward (first-to-last within 100 MB); VoiceBox footprint grows at most 3000 MB; slot dir ≤ 2; controller alive and IDLE.
6. **Actual behavior:** FAIL at the time of writing, on two gate artifacts that were then fixed (see 9–11), plus a genuine VoiceBox crash (see 10/18). The controller itself passed every invariant in every run: 24/24 turns played in each soak, zero failed speech, mic deltas 0 in both quiet runs, controller alive and IDLE afterwards, footprint 470 MB (peak 522 MB) after 12 soak cycles.
7. **Evidence:** `.local/voice-agent/runs/VA-SOAK-006-LONG-run1.json` (run 1), `.local/voice-agent/runs/VA-SOAK-006-LONG-run5.json` (run 5), `.local/voice-agent/runs/VA-SOAK-006-LONG.json` (latest run). Idle RSS trace: 40 samples at 3 s over 120 s on pid 38298 (min 211.5, max 341.0, phys_footprint 336–472 MB); three independent 120 s medians: 272.2 / 328.4 / 360.2 MB.
8. **Level:** LEVEL 3 (live services, real playback, real controller; no microphone input exercised — the probe never opens the mic).
9. **What was reproduced first:** run 1 FAILed on three separate probe-design bugs: (a) the mic gate read lifetime-cumulative counters (`mic_dropped` 7 at run end, all pre-existing); (b) the RSS baseline was a single `ps` sample (148.6 MB) landing on a low phase of the documented ±200 MB residency oscillation, compared against a post-run median of 259.7 MB; (c) two human wakes during the window ("Hey Jarvis." at 19:56:32 and 19:57:56 local, score 0.80) epoch-aborted cycle 6 turn 3's speech (`dropped reason:'abort'`) and were scored as controller failures.
10. **Root causes found:**
   - Turn attribution credited *any* afplay pid to the current turn, so a chunked reply's later clips were charged to the next turn (cycle 4 recorded 4/4 "played" while only 2 clips were generated; six 0.0 s latencies were artifacts). `speech.log` rotates to `speech.log.1` at 1 MB (`SPEECH_LOG_LIMIT` in `extension/voicebox-speech.ts`), so mid-run events vanished from the primary file; attribution now merges `speech.log` + `speech.log.1`.
   - `voicebox_footprint_mb()` picked the first listener, which can be the KB-scale GUI launcher (`Footprint: 2400 KB`), and returned `None` on transient lsof misses. It now takes the largest listener footprint with one retry.
   - The 20 MB settle limit is below the instrument noise band: three independent 120 s medians of the *idle* controller spread 88 MB. Absolute RSS drift below ~100 MB is unresolvable while the ~46 GiB model is resident. The gate is now a 100 MB band plus a rising-floor trend check (first-to-last cycle median), which churn cannot fake but a real leak can.
   - **Genuine finding:** the VoiceBox sidecar (`voicebox-server`, pid 38531) SIGABRT-crashed at 21:37:15 local on 2026-11-14 (`~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports/voicebox-server-2026-11-14-213715/213716.ips`, abort trap 6, both launcher and worker) after its footprint climbed from ~5.6 GB to ~8.2–9.0 GB during repeated soak generations. Nothing listens on 17493 afterwards; per the runbook only a human reopening VoiceBox recovers it. This is the first measured TTS-load crash, and it corroborates the 3000 MB VoiceBox growth gate.
11. **Fix:** probe gates changed to: mic deltas vs run-start snapshot; median-of-24 RSS baseline and post-run median (100 MB settle band, 300 MB envelope ceiling); per-cycle 6-sample medians with a trend gate; strict per-turn clip attribution from merged speech logs; VoiceBox max-listener footprint with retry; three-valued verdict (`PASS` / `CONTAMINATED` exit 3 / `FAIL` exit 1). No gate was weakened below measurable reality — the 100 MB band is the *measured* noise floor, and the trend gate adds a stronger leak signal than the old absolute one.
12. **Tests run:** `pytest tests/test_long_soak_verdict.py -q` → 8 passed (healthy PASS, lifetime mic counts do not fail a healthy delta, human speech ⇒ CONTAMINATED, wake-only ⇒ FAIL, foreign-session playback ⇒ CONTAMINATED, median settle/envelope limits, rising floor ⇒ FAIL, oscillating floor ⇒ PASS, missing baseline ⇒ FAIL). Full suites: 297 passed (Python), 110 passed (Node). Live probe: 5 runs (see history).
13. **Result:** run 5 (2026-11-15 18:33–19:06 UTC) CONTAMINATED — every controller invariant passed (24/24 turns attributed to their own clips, zero wake, zero failed speech, mic deltas 0/0, slot dir 1, controller IDLE, RSS 289.1 → 149.0 MB median with a −208.3 MB trend, VoiceBox 3268 → peak 4262 MB inside the 3000 MB gate). Two instrumentation gates tripped, neither behavioral: the VoiceBox baseline footprint read returned null on transient lsof misses at run start, and `render_connected` was true at the final snapshot because the owner's live session 56658 played 53 clips concurrently during the window. Fixes shipped: footprint read retries 4×; new `external_generated_lines` gate classifies concurrent foreign sessions as CONTAMINATED instead of FAIL. Clean PASS awaits a rerun in a quiet window.
14. **Latencies measured:** quiet run 4 first-audio latencies per cycle: 47.12 / 14.74 / 11.7 / 15.39; 17.96 / 12.9 / 0.01 / 12.48; 20.07 / 21.18 / 10.86 / 15.93; 22.53 / 10.39 / 10.59 / 4.32; 19.15 / 9.45 / 3.96 / 8.66; 16.14 / 9.77 / 0.31 / 9.42 s. Model contention inflates turn 1 of every cycle.
15. **Boundary behavior:** the 300 MB envelope never fired (run 4 max sample 327.7 MB vs baseline median 282.2). The VoiceBox 3000 MB gate would have fired in run 3 (5659 → 8983 MB) had that run not been contaminated.
16. **Cleanup:** the probe terminates each omp session and closes its pty master; evidence JSONs stay in `.local/voice-agent/runs/`.
17. **Automation status:** LEVEL 3 live probe, rerunnable any time; no microphone opened.
18. **Known limitations:**
   - Human speech during the window is a `CONTAMINATED` verdict, not a failure — rerunning requires a quiet window (both contaminated runs came from the owner dictating into the live session).
   - `mic_dropped` rose by 5 during the contaminated run (queue-full drops when the main loop is busy); quiet runs showed delta 0. The cause is not yet root-caused.
   - Session-registry entries accumulate (3 → 9 across run 2) because pruning is lazy (`load_session_registry` unlinks only dead-pid entries when it next reads); ungated by design.
   - `ps` RSS remains an imprecise instrument under pressure; the trend gate mitigates but cannot resolve drift under 100 MB.
   - VoiceBox is healthy again (sidecar pid 63081, 0.6B mlx/cpu, baseline 3268 MB, VA-VB-001 PASS); a clean PASS is now gated only on a quiet window, not on the app being reopened.
19. **Last verified:** 2026-11-15 19:25 UTC, controller pid 38298, VoiceBox sidecar 63081, working tree on `16ee173` plus uncommitted fixes.
